# Karena AI: Enterprise RAG Knowledge Intelligence Platform

### TL;DR
Karena AI is an enterprise-grade, cloud-native retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) knowledge assistant designed to unify fragmented information silos, synthesize actionable intelligence, and drive rapid, authoritative decision-making for large-scale organizations. Addressing business-critical pain points such as knowledge fragmentation and operational inefficiency, Karena AI empowers enterprises—especially in the Asia-Pacific region’s financial, healthcare, and technology sectors—by delivering sub-second, verifiable insights that elevate productivity and compliance standards.

---

## Goals

### Business Goals

- Achieve a 30% reduction in mean-time-to-resolution (MTTR) for technical support queries by Q2 2025.
- Accelerate employee knowledge discovery workflows by 40% within the first year post-deployment.
- Decrease redundant documentation maintenance by 25% across target departments in the first 18 months.
- Attain at least 99.9% service uptime in line with APAC enterprise SLA benchmarks.
- Deliver demonstrable ROI within 12 months as measured via reduced support tickets, improved decision velocity, and savings from operational consolidation.

### User Goals

- Seamless retrieval and authoritative synthesis of cross-domain knowledge via natural language interfaces.
- Multi-turn conversational context with reliable session memory and source traceability.
- Productivity boost through reduced search friction and fewer escalations to expert teams.
- Trust in response accuracy, security, and compliance with organizational and regulatory mandates.

### Non-Goals

- Direct authoring or editing of source knowledge bases from the AI interface.
- Real-time transactional automations outside the knowledge search and synthesis domain.
- Consumer-market deployment scenarios (focus is strictly large enterprises and regulated verticals).

## User Experience
**Entry Point & First-Time User Experience**

- Users discover Karena AI through enterprise portals, SSO-enabled web apps, or integrations (e.g., Microsoft Teams, Slack, ServiceNow).
- Onboarding includes a context-driven tour of search, source traceability, and escalation capabilities, with tooltips and quick start videos.

**Core Experience**

- **Step 1:** User submits a natural-language query via web, chat, or mobile endpoint.Autocomplete suggests relevant recent queries and document types.
- Input validation for intent and domain coverage.

- **Step 2:** System orchestrates semantic retrieval, applies context memory and re-ranking.All processing is transparent; status indicators and real-time progress bars shown for longer queries.

- **Step 3:** Response panel details synthesized answer, source citations, and confidence indicators.Users can expand to view underlying documents, trace chain-of-reasoning, or refine queries.

- **Step 4:** Multi-turn dialogue is supported, resolving pronouns/contexts and surfacing related insights.Interactions are persisted for later retrieval and longitudinal analysis.

- **Step 5:** For complex, unresolved, or high-risk queries, the system escalates to designated human experts with the full query context attached.User receives real-time status updates and resolution timelines.

**Advanced Features & Edge Cases**

- Power users configure prompt templates or define retrieval agent policies.
- Edge cases: incomplete source data, ambiguous queries, rate-limited sessions, and compliance event triggers—all with graceful fallback or notification UX.

**UI/UX Highlights**

- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility (keyboard navigation, contrast, screen-reader-ready markup)
- Responsive web and mobile layouts; localization for multi-country APAC deployments
- Direct export to productivity suites (G Suite, Office 365)
- Tenant-based theming and branding.

## Success Metrics

- 30% reduction in support ticket mean resolution time (tracked via ticketing system integration).
- 40% acceleration in information retrieval for end-users (measured by workflow analytics, timed user studies).
- 25% reduction in duplicate documentation maintenance effort (measured via content audit before/after).
- 99.9%+ uptime and 1024 dims increases storage and nearest-neighbor compute by roughly 4–8x depending on index structure and compression.
- Cache hit rates: improving cache hit from 60% -> 90% can reduce inference OPEX by a large fraction—fewer model calls, lower tail latency, and reduced autoscaling.
- Model selection: switching from a large LLM to a distilled retrieval-optimized model for low-risk queries can cut per-query inference cost by an order of magnitude while preserving acceptable fidelity for many enterprise use cases.

**Scenario-based monthly illustrative table (narrative)**: Low utilization: Minimal GPU footprint, aggressive preemptible usage, compact retention windows—optimized monthly OPEX focused on storage & baseline orchestration. Medium utilization: Mixed GPU fleet, committed-use discounts applied, balanced retention and realtime indexing—moderate inference & storage costs. High utilization: Full GPU fleet with redundancy, multi-region replication for low latency, long retention—highest monthly OPEX with emphasis on committed contracts and architectural optimization to manage spend.

**Optimization levers & fiscal governance**: Encourage committed-use commitments for baseline capacity, leverage preemptible/spot VMs for batch embedding and retraining, implement multi-tier caching and embedding quantization to reduce storage and compute, employ model routing (small model for routine queries, large model for high-risk/high-value interactions), and regional placement of compute to minimize egress. Establish continuous cost telemetry (per-query cost, per-corpus storage cost, cache effectiveness) and monthly budget reviews tied to usage forecasts.
